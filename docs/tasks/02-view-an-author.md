# View an author

**Status:** Not built

## Who this is for

A signed-in person needs to open one author and read the record the library keeps.

## Requirement

Anyone who is signed in can open a single author that is already on file.

## Information

The view shows the name, biography, year of birth, and nationality saved for that author. Empty optional fields are shown as empty.

## Who may do this

- Member, librarian, and administrator: yes
- Anyone who is not signed in: no

## Rules

- The author must already exist.
- An unknown author is refused, and the reason says the author was not found.
- Opening an author does not change the record.

## Done when

- A member, a librarian, and an administrator can each open an author and see the saved name and optional details.
- Anyone who is not signed in is refused.
- An unknown author is refused, and no author record changes.
